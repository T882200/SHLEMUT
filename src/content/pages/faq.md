## Frequently Asked Questions

### Purchasing

**How do I purchase a product?**
Browse our [shop](/SHLEMUT/shop/), add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Payment is handled securely through our payment provider.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay through our payment partner.

**Will I receive a receipt?**
Yes, a detailed receipt is sent to your email immediately after purchase.

### Downloads & Delivery

**How do I receive my purchase?**
After payment, you'll receive an email with download links and serial keys (if applicable). Downloads are available immediately.

**How long are download links valid?**
Download links are valid for 30 days after purchase. If your link expires, contact support for a new one.

**Can I re-download my purchases?**
Yes, contact support with your order number and we'll issue new download links.

### Fonts

**What formats are included?**
Font purchases include `.otf`, `.ttf`, and `.woff2` formats suitable for desktop and web use.

**Can I use fonts on websites?**
Yes, our standard license includes web font usage for up to 250,000 monthly page views. Contact us for extended licensing.

**Do fonts include Hebrew characters?**
Character set support varies by font. Check each product page for specific language support details.

### Scripts

**How do I install InDesign scripts?**
After downloading, place the `.jsx` file in your InDesign Scripts folder. On macOS: `~/Library/Preferences/Adobe InDesign/[version]/Scripts/`. On Windows: `C:\Users\[user]\AppData\Roaming\Adobe\InDesign\[version]\Scripts\`.

**What InDesign versions are supported?**
Each script lists its minimum supported version. Most scripts work with InDesign CC 2019 and later.

**How do serial keys work?**
Scripts that require activation will prompt for your serial key on first use. Enter the key from your purchase email to unlock full functionality.

### Licensing

**What does the standard license cover?**
Our standard license permits use in unlimited personal and commercial projects for a single user. See our full [License Terms](/SHLEMUT/license/) for details.

**Can I share my license with my team?**
Each license is for a single user. For team licensing, contact us for volume pricing.

### Support

**How do I get help?**
Email us at support@shlemut.com with your order number and a description of your issue. We typically respond within 24 hours.

**I found a bug in a script. What should I do?**
Please email us with your InDesign version, operating system, and steps to reproduce the issue. We take bug reports seriously and release fixes promptly.
