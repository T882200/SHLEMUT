## Our Story

SHLEMUT was born from a simple belief: designers deserve tools that are as thoughtfully crafted as the work they create.

We specialize in **premium fonts** and **InDesign automation scripts** — digital assets built by designers, for designers. Every product in our catalog is the result of meticulous attention to detail, extensive testing, and a deep understanding of professional design workflows.

## What We Offer

### Typefaces
Our font collection focuses on quality over quantity. Each typeface is designed with comprehensive character sets, carefully tuned spacing, and professional-grade OpenType features. We have a special focus on **Hebrew-Latin** typefaces that maintain visual harmony across both scripts.

### InDesign Scripts
Our automation scripts tackle the repetitive tasks that slow designers down. From table formatting to page numbering, each script is built to save you hours while maintaining the precision your work demands.

## Our Principles

- **Quality first** — We'd rather release one exceptional product than ten mediocre ones
- **Designer-focused** — Built by people who use these tools daily
- **Fair pricing** — Premium quality doesn't have to mean premium pricing
- **Responsive support** — We stand behind every product we sell
