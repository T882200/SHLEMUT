## PageNumberer Pro — Smart Page Numbering

Take complete control over page numbering in Adobe InDesign. PageNumberer Pro handles everything from simple sequential numbering to complex multi-section books with mixed formats.

### Features

- **Multiple formats** — Arabic, Roman, alphabetic, custom patterns
- **Section support** — Independent numbering per section
- **Prefix/suffix** — Add custom text around numbers
- **Skip pages** — Exclude specific pages from numbering
- **Position control** — Place numbers anywhere with precision
- **Master page aware** — Respects your master page layouts
- **Book support** — Synchronize numbering across InDesign Book files

### Use Cases

- Academic publications with frontmatter (i, ii, iii) and body (1, 2, 3)
- Magazine layouts with section-specific numbering
- Legal documents requiring precise page references
- Multi-chapter book projects

### System Requirements

- Adobe InDesign CC 2019 or later
- macOS 10.14+ or Windows 10+
