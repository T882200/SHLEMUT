## Narkis Display — Bold Statement Typography

Narkis Display is a high-contrast display typeface that brings drama and elegance to your headlines. With its sharp serifs and generous proportions, it's designed to make an immediate visual impact.

### Key Features

- **High contrast** — Dramatic thick-thin strokes
- **3 weights** — Regular, Bold, and Black
- **Optimized for large sizes** — Perfect at 48px and above
- **Latin script** — Full Western European character support
- **Stylistic alternates** — Decorative swash capitals included

### Best For

- Magazine covers and editorial spreads
- Poster and event design
- Brand headlines
- Social media graphics
- Packaging with premium feel

### What's Included

- `.otf` files for all 3 weights
- `.woff2` web font files
- Specimen PDF with usage guidelines
